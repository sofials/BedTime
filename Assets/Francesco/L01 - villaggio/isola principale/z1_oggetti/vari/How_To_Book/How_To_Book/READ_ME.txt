Description:
A witty How To book titled, How To Lead in a Field You Know Nothing About

Included Files:
- FBX, OBJ, and BLEND versions of the model
- PBR Textures (2K resolution, PNG) for Unreal Engine and Blender
- Previews for reference

Model Details:
- Polycount: 88 faces / 172 tris
- Non-overlapping UVs
- Single texture set
- Real-world scale (1 unit = 1 meter)

Texture Maps:
- BaseColor
- Normal
- Roughness
- Metallic
- Ambient Occlusion
- Emissive

Exported at 2K resolution. All textures in .PNG format.

Recommended Use:
- Works in Unreal Engine, Unity, Blender, Maya, and most 3D apps.
- To use in Unreal: import model and connect maps via packed or unpacked material setup. For OcclusionRoughnessMetallic texture, Blue channel goes to Metallic, Green channel goes to Roughness, Red channel goes to Ambient Occlusion
- To use in Unity: invert Roughness.
